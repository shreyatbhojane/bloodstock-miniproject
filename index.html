Take a look inside

BloodStock is a simple blood bank inventory management demonstration for organizing stock records, reviewing blood groups, and monitoring expiry dates.

You can review inventory summaries, add and edit batches, search and combine filters, delete records with confirmation, and inspect expiry information. Settings let you adjust the low-stock threshold and alert window.

Yes. The demo displays expired records and upcoming dates, with a configurable overview alert window and 3-day and 7-day tracker views. Actual inventory handling must follow your organization’s approved procedures.

No. This is a standalone frontend demonstration. It does not connect to hospital, laboratory, or external blood bank systems and does not provide live blood availability.

The dashboard uses browser LocalStorage. Records remain in the same browser profile until cleared or reset. They are not shared between devices or users. This page’s illustrative previews do not change your saved inventory.

Yes. Download all records matching your inventory or report filters as a CSV file. The Reports view also includes a Print Report action with a clean print layout.
